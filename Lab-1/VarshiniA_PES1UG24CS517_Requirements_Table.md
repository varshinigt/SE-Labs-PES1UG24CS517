**Problem Statement:** #28 - Inter-City Freight Load Matching Marketplace

**Scenario:** A B2B freight brokerage exchange where commercial shippers post cargo loads and trucking carriers submit bids, with automated milestone payment release upon proof-of-delivery sign-off.

| Req ID | Type | Description | Priority | Acceptance Criteria | Rationale | Comments |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FR-001 | Functional | The system shall allow verified trucking carriers to submit competitive bids on posted freight shipments within a specified auction timeframe. | High | **Pass:** a bid submitted by a verified carrier during the specified auction timeframe is recorded and the shipper is notified. **Fail:** an unverified carrier is able to submit a bid, or a bid submitted after the auction timeframe is accepted. | Bidding is the core marketplace interaction between shippers and carriers. | |
| FR-002 | Functional | The system shall allow a shipper to post a freight load by entering origin, destination, cargo type, weight, and pickup and delivery time windows. | High | **Pass:** after all required fields are submitted, the load is published and visible to verified carriers. **Fail:** a load with any required field missing is published. | Carriers need complete shipment details before deciding whether to bid. | |
| FR-003 | Functional | The system shall allow a shipper to review submitted bids and award a load to exactly one carrier before the auction deadline. If no bid is awarded, the system shall automatically close the auction as unawarded at the deadline. | High | **Pass:** selecting `Award Bid` before the deadline locks the auction, notifies the winning and losing carriers, and sets the load status to `Awarded`. If no bid is selected, the auction closes automatically at the deadline as `Closed - Unawarded`. **Fail:** more than one carrier is awarded the same load, or an unawarded auction remains open after its deadline. | Each shipment needs one responsible carrier, and every auction must reach a clear final state. | Peer review: clarified the valid unawarded outcome and automatic deadline closure. |
| FR-004 | Functional | The system shall allow the awarded carrier to submit an electronic proof of delivery (e-POD) containing a digital signature, timestamp, and GPS geo-tag after completing delivery. | High | **Pass:** the awarded carrier submits an e-POD containing all three required components; it is recorded and the shipment status changes to `Delivered - Pending Verification`. **Fail:** a non-awarded carrier can submit the e-POD, or an e-POD missing any required component is accepted. | A complete e-POD from the awarded carrier is required before delivery can be verified and payment can be released. | |
| FR-005 | Functional | The system shall allow shippers and carriers to view the current status of any load they are associated with, including `Posted`, `Awarded`, `In Transit`, `Delivered - Pending Verification`, `Delivered - Payment Released`, `Payment Release Failed - Manual Review`, and `Closed - Unawarded`. | Medium | **Pass:** an associated shipper or carrier can view the load status, and the displayed status matches the backend state within 15 seconds of a status change. **Fail:** an unrelated user can view the load, or the displayed status is more than 15 seconds out of date. | Status visibility reduces uncertainty while restricting shipment information to involved parties. | |
| NFR-001 | Nonfunctional | The system shall automatically disburse milestone payments held in escrow to the carrier within 60 seconds of a verified electronic proof-of-delivery submission, and payment-release communication with the Payment Gateway shall be encrypted. | High | **Pass:** under simulated peak load, a verified e-POD results in payment disbursement within 60 seconds of its submission and Payment Gateway communication is encrypted. **Fail:** payment takes longer than 60 seconds, or Payment Gateway communication is not encrypted. | Fast, secure payout is important to carrier trust and protects payment data. | |
| NFR-002 | Nonfunctional | The system shall maintain at least 99.9% availability for load posting, bidding, and shipment tracking over each calendar month. | Medium | **Pass:** monthly availability logs show at least 99.9% uptime for load posting, bidding, and shipment tracking. **Fail:** monthly availability for any of those services is below 99.9%. | High availability helps prevent missed bids and interruptions to active shipments. | |
